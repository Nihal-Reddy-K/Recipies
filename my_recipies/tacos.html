Taco Bake Casserole

Home

Description:

This taco bake is quick and easy and will satisfy even the most picky eaters! You can modify this simple casserole to add things you love. It's yummy! Top with tomatoes, lettuce, salsa, or whatever you like on your Mexican food.

Ingredients:

  • Beef
  • Salsa
  • Olives
  • Refried Beans
  • Green Chiles

Steps:

  1. Cook the ground beef and onions until beef is browned. Drain the grease and add water and taco seasoning. Cook until the water and seasoning mixture has thickened.
  2. Add refried beans, olives, green chiles, and salsa to the ground beef mixture. Cook until all the ingredients are warmed through.
  3. Sprinkle broken taco shells over the bottom of a casserole dish. Top with a layer of ground beef mixture, then a layer of cheese. Repeat all three layers.
  4. Bake the casserole until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
